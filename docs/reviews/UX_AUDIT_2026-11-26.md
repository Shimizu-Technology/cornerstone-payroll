# Usability audit: employee app, client portal, accountant side

**Date:** Nov 26, 2026 (Guam)
**Tested build:** `13272ac05`. `origin/main` has since moved to `cebfa52c1` (#598, database enforcement), which changes nothing under `web/`.
**Method:** an agent walked first-time-user tasks in headless Chromium (Playwright) at 390×844 (phone) and 1440×900 (desktop), using fake data only. No production system was touched.
**Scope:** findings only. Payroll feature names and their places on the accountant side are left as Cornerstone knows them. This audit covers wording, guidance, empty states, errors, next steps, phone layout, consistency and discoverability.

Ratings:

- **P1:** blocks or confuses most users.
- **P2:** slows down or confuses some users.
- **P3:** polish.

Effort:

- **XS:** under an hour.
- **S:** about half a day.
- **M:** one to two days.

## Summary

The employee app is in good shape. A first-time employee can find their latest pay, open a stub, download the PDF, find the W-2GU and get help without guidance. The worst friction is elsewhere:

1. **Accountant: a new hire's first payroll can't be approved, and nothing says where to fix it** (A1). Two required new-hire documents are never mentioned until **Approve** fails. The error names them but doesn't link to them. The upload sits at the bottom of the Edit form, behind **Manage documents**.
2. **Client manager: "review and approve" is a dead end** (C1). The open period's page says to "review and approve the preview on the pay run". It has no link, and the client's **Pay Periods** list doesn't show the open period at all.
3. **Errors that say the wrong thing.** One example: **Changes to confirm** says "This record could not be found. Refresh the list…" when the feature is simply off (A5). Several other screens give no next step when something is off or empty (A8, C12, C14).

The recommended fix order is at the end, as seven small PR batches.

## 1. Employee app (`web/src/employee/`)

What works well:

- **Home:** leads with take-home pay, payment status and **View pay stub**.
- **Hide amounts:** explains itself once.
- **Stub:** shows hours × rate and labels taxes "Guam income tax", never "Federal".
- **PDF:** downloads as `pay-stub-2026-11-20.pdf`.
- **Desktop Pay screen:** the list beside the stub reads well.
- **Can't sign in?:** says what to do.
- **Sign-up mismatch:** the message is generic and safe: "We couldn't match those details… After too many tries, sign-up pauses for 24 hours".
- **Confirm-it's-you:** cancelling says "Not saved. To save your phone number, confirm it's you."
- **Former employees:** see "You're viewing your past pay … until Feb 15, 2027. Nothing here can be changed."

### E1 · P2 · Step 1 of sign-up looks checked, but nothing is checked until step 2

- **Screen:** Set up your account, Step 1 of 2, then Step 2 (Your email).
- **What happens:** Step 1 says "First, let's check it's you." A wrong date of birth still moves on to Step 2. The employee types an email, ticks the Terms box and taps **Create account**. Only then are they sent back to Step 1 with "We couldn't match those details."
- **Why it confuses:** the screen promises a check that doesn't happen. The employee does Step 2 for nothing and may think their email caused the problem.
- **Fix:** run the match check when Step 1's **Continue** is tapped. If one check per attempt is a deliberate rate-limit choice, change Step 1's copy to "First, tell us who you are. We'll check everything when you create your account." Either way, keep the Step 2 entries when the person is sent back.
- **Files:** `web/src/employee/signup.tsx` (activation call near line 289); `api/app/controllers/api/v1/employee_portal/activations_controller.rb` if checking at Step 1.
- **Effort:** S for the copy, M for checking at Step 1.

### E2 · P2 · On a phone, Sign out is at the very bottom of My info

- **Screen:** every signed-in screen at 390 px.
- **What happens:** desktop shows **Sign out** in the top bar. On a phone the only **Sign out** is at the end of My info, below both electronic-delivery disclosures, several screens down.
- **Why it confuses:** people on a shared or borrowed phone look for Sign out at the top or under an account menu.
- **Fix:** add a small account button in the phone header (name, Switch employer, Sign out), or move **Sign out** to the top of My info.
- **Files:** `web/src/employee/Shell.tsx`, `web/src/employee/myinfo.tsx`, `web/src/employee/SignOut.tsx`.
- **Effort:** S.

### E3 · P2 · My info shows both full consent disclosures inline, and says "pilot"

- **Screen:** My info, the **Electronic pay statements** and **Electronic Form W-2GU** sections.
- **What happens:** each section prints its whole disclosure, with the checkbox and button below it. Both say "Paper copies continue for everyone during the pilot."
- **Why it confuses:**
  - My info becomes very long, which hides Sign out (E2).
  - "Pilot" is an internal word, so employees may wonder whether the app is temporary.
- **Fix:**
  - Collapse each disclosure behind "Read the disclosure", with the status ("Paper delivery") visible.
  - Replace "during the pilot" with "for now" or "Harbor Finch Kitchen still mails paper copies."
- **Files:** `web/src/employee/consent.tsx`.
- **Effort:** S.

## 2. Client manager / HR portal (client routes on the staff site)

What works well:

- Contact Details helps: the **Needs contact details** filter, the "+1 671 is added for you" hint and inline errors.
- **Employee app settings** has a live preview and a contrast check ("White text on your color: 9.0 to 1").
- **Notifications** is clear about email being off.
- Phone layouts at 390 px have no horizontal scrolling on any screen tested.

### C1 · P1 · "Review and approve" leads nowhere

- **Screen:** **Pay Period Files**, then the open period (e.g. Nov 8–21). Also **Pay Periods**.
- **What happens:**
  - The open period says "Review and approve the preview on the pay run." It has no link to a pay run.
  - **Pay Periods** lists only committed and imported periods, so the open period isn't there.
  - Nothing says whether this client's approval is needed or when a preview will be ready.
- **Why it confuses:** reviewing and approving is the client manager's main task. Following the instruction leads nowhere.
- **Fix:**
  - On the period page, show one status line: "Cornerstone is preparing this payroll. We'll let you know when a preview is ready," or **Open payroll preview** once one exists.
  - Show the open period at the top of **Pay Periods** with a plain status ("Being prepared", "Ready for your review", "Finalized").
  - Remove the sentence when this client doesn't approve payroll.
- **Files:** `web/src/pages/payroll-submissions/PayrollPeriodPage.tsx`, `web/src/pages/client/ClientPayPeriods.tsx`, `web/src/pages/client/ClientPayPeriodDetail.tsx`.
- **Effort:** M.

### C2 · P2 · Three overlapping places for files and messages

- **Screen:** sidebar: **Pay Period Files**, **Messages**, **Documents**.
- **What happens:** each period page has its own messages. **Messages** holds conversations. **Documents** has an upload form and a second full message composer. The dashboard adds **Upload Documents**.
- **Why it confuses:** a manager doesn't know where to send this week's timesheet or where Cornerstone's reply will appear.
- **Fix:**
  - Give each page one job. **Documents** is for files only, without the composer. **Messages** holds every conversation. A period page shows that period's files and links to its conversation.
  - Add a one-line subtitle on each page saying what goes there.
- **Files:** `web/src/pages/client/ClientDocuments.tsx`, `web/src/pages/client/ClientMessages.tsx`, `web/src/pages/payroll-submissions/PayrollPeriodPage.tsx`.
- **Effort:** M.

### C3 · P2 · The same payroll has different status words on different pages

- **What happens:** **Pay Periods** says "Committed" (or "Locked" for QuickBooks imports). **Pay Period Files** calls the same periods "Paid". The dashboard says "Committed payrolls ready to review".
- **Why it confuses:** "Committed" is payroll-staff vocabulary. "Ready to review" next to a finished payroll suggests the client still has something to do.
- **Fix:** use one client-facing set: **Being prepared**, **Ready for your review**, **Finalized**, **Imported (read-only)**. Change "ready to review" to "finalized payrolls". The accountant side keeps its own terms.
- **Files:** `web/src/pages/client/ClientPayPeriods.tsx`, `web/src/pages/client/ClientDashboard.tsx`, `web/src/pages/payroll-submissions/PayrollPeriodsList.tsx`.
- **Effort:** S.

### C4 · P2 · The dashboard doesn't say what needs doing

- **Screen:** client **Dashboard**.
- **What happens:**
  - The top action is **Add Employee**.
  - "Current payroll" showed a period whose pay date (Nov 13) had already passed, while the Nov 8–21 period was open.
  - "YTD Payroll $161,222.30" is net pay, but the label doesn't say so. Reports shows gross $189,007.09 for the same data.
- **Fix:**
  - Lead with a "Next payroll" card: the period, the pay date, the status and the one action needed (send files, review the preview, or nothing).
  - Rename the figure **YTD net pay**, or show gross and net.
  - On phones, put this card above the stat tiles.
- **Files:** `web/src/pages/client/ClientDashboard.tsx`.
- **Effort:** M.

### A1 · P1 · A new hire's first payroll can't be approved, and nothing says where to fix it

- **Screen:** Add Employee, then the pay run, **Calculate**, **Approve**.
- **What happens:**
  - Juniper Moss (fake) was added with every required field. The success screen and the employee Overview didn't mention documents. In Results, Juniper's **Flags** column showed "—".

- **Fix (feature names and order stay the same):**
  - In the approval error, make each employee name a link that opens their documents manager.
  - In Enter Hours and Results, flag the row: "2 documents needed".
  - On the employee Overview, add a card: "Before this employee's first payroll: ID and work authorization, signed W-4", with **Upload or verify**.
  - After **Create Employee**, say: "Employee created. Upload their ID and signed W-4 before their first payroll is approved."
- **Files:**
  - `api/app/services/employee_document_readiness.rb:73` (message; add employee IDs to the payload)
  - `web/src/pages/pay-periods/PayRunWorkspace.tsx`
  - `web/src/pages/employees/EmployeeWorkspace.tsx`
