# Employee setup and special payroll runs

The retained Payroll/AIRE audit found stale hourly rates reused as annual salary, new employees defaulting to the wrong client frequency, special runs with no clear employee selection, missing field-label associations, and poor recovery when incomplete-entry permission expired. This repair addresses F02, F03, F04, F05 and F09, plus the EmployeeForm portion of F13.

Current hourly definitions remain in history when an employee changes to salary or a flat contractor fee. New/current calculations use the current pay basis; stale hourly worksheet payloads reject with reload guidance. Correction references retain their original scalar. Separate source-integrity work in PR331 supplies the retained-source and manual-reference compatibility guard; this repair does not invent historical payroll evidence.

New forms follow the selected client frequency until the operator chooses an override. Input labels are associated with stable distinct controls. Incomplete-entry expiry returns a clear not-saved explanation and field errors, refreshes policy, and retains the entered draft. Direct forms in a reviewer workspace show read-only recovery without dropping the draft. PR333 supplies the canonical effective company permissions.

Fresh special runs ask the operator to choose employees before calculating. The worksheet and estimates include only selected workers. Remove from this run clears that worker’s staged hours and pay amounts; choosing them again starts clean. Mobile selection and calculation controls have at least 44px height and wrap within the viewport.

A second salary worker calculated $2,166.67 semimonthly. New-form frequency was semimonthly; an explicit weekly override persisted after an expired-intake rejection, retained-draft completion and successful retry. Rejection created no employee. All visible labels had associated controls; clicking First Name focused that field on desktop. The initial fixture’s foreign department association was corrected before accepted salary-save evidence; the rejected setup attempts are retained locally.

No approvals, commits, real payments or provider actions were performed by these cases.
