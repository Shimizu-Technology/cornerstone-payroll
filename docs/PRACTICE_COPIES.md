# Practice copies and client portal rehearsal

An administrator can create a Test workspace from a production client and choose the last committed payroll to copy, or leave out recent payrolls. Copied payrolls are locked reference history. New payrolls can be calculated and reviewed inside the copy; payroll cannot be committed there.

Copies preserve promoted payrolls through `test_workspace_source_pay_period_id`, without retaining their promotion or payment-preparation authority. Each baseline item's decimal financial values are verified. Applied opening YTD is copied as an immutable bridge snapshot, including all employee balance fields. The workspace manifest identifies the source batch, bridge and revision. Upload adapter choices and client approval settings are inherited. Original import files, messages, payment records and external connections are not copied. A cutoff before the opening YTD boundary fails without retaining a partial copy. Setup-only copies retain the current applied opening.

To rehearse the client side, invite a **Client Portal User** with a separate email and select one ready practice copy. Selecting it clears real-client selections. The resulting identity is permanently tied to that copy: it cannot become staff, move to a real client, or gain another company assignment. An existing real-client identity cannot be assigned to a practice copy. The portal displays **Practice copy**, and staff see labelled practice entries in Client Inbox only when they have workspace access.

The invitation uses the normal sign-in invitation delivery. Practice activity never sends portal notifications or payroll reminders. Practice clients cannot become real billing recipients, invoice sources or financial books. Existing guards continue to block payroll commits, real checks, payments, tax deposits, filing-ready actions and external synchronization. Plain-paper draft payroll PDFs and the existing VOID-marked check preview remain available; neither allocates real check numbers or records payment.

Archiving deactivates practice portal identities in the same transaction and records a security audit. Expiry denies access immediately at authentication, and `ExpirePracticePortalAccessJob` persists deactivation every minute. Removing the practice assignment also denies access. Restore preserves records and staff grants; it does not reactivate client identities. An administrator must explicitly reactivate a restored practice identity. Active account, unexpired assignment, ready workspace and organization checks still apply.

The client may upload payroll files, exchange messages, review posted draft PDFs and approve a draft. Client approval does not commit payroll or cause payment. Archive retains the rehearsal evidence inside the isolated copy; it does not delete or merge it into production-client history.
