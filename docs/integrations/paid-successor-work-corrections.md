# Changed work after financial succession

An accepted whole-payroll financial correction can move the payment obligation to a new payroll item while the original imported work stays frozen on its original allocation. A later exact negative Source work correction must use that frozen allocation as work evidence and the current paid successor as its accounting target.

Payroll resolves every allocation in the original paycheck's complete frozen union through its accepted financial links. Each allocation must follow the same unique chain to one current owner. The resolver checks the installed Source identity, immutable command and accepted receipt, source entry version, native reversal and correction audit, current REG/OT totals and historical rate, and the current owner's actual check delivery or bank confirmation. Pending, disconnected, foreign or mixed ownership remains held for review.

The preview binds the frozen union, accepted chain, current target, payment evidence and calculated adjustment. Confirmation locks the company, import, chain, relevant periods and items, and payment evidence, then revalidates the proof before writing. Only the exact negative REG/OT correction can use the internal accounting exception for a committed, active correction run. The ordinary public corrective-paycheck predicate remains unchanged.

A 4 to 3 REG correction at $25 per hour against an issued $100 gross / $92.35 net successor records a supplemental of −1 REG, −$25 gross, −$1.91 deductions and −$23.09 net. It assigns no check and creates no bank payment or recovery. Settled checks, frozen imports, allocations and financial links remain unchanged. The signed exact line can be consumed once. The paid target and its supplemental remain protected by the existing accounting disposition void guards.

The existing Source receipt contract needs no change. Its `original_pay_period_id` and `original_payroll_item_id` identify the paid parent of the supplemental; Source compares those IDs with the latest exact issuance on the original frozen batch. Payroll separately retains the frozen origin and full succession proof in the supplemental's existing calculation context and shows both owners in its preview and recorded result. No Source code, protocol, endpoint, schema or capability is added.

Older accounting dispositions whose target is their original paid allocation owner retain their existing verification path. A successor disposition requires the saved chain proof. Missing or changed evidence remains visible for review; the workflow does not repair production history or infer an owner from the newest row alone.

Automated coverage does not establish paired GUI acceptance. Desktop and narrow-screen review, actual Source correction/reapproval/export, receipt delivery and saved downstream outcomes remain required in the separate acceptance workflow.

Posted target evidence uses version 2. It retains every payroll-item and pay-period column except the explicitly listed operational fields in `PaidCorrectionTarget`: tax-sync status, attempts, request key, error and completion time; row update timestamps; the clerical pay/check dates; and the seven item YTD snapshots that the native pay-date correction rebuilds. It separately binds the target's tax year. Current wages, withholding and employer taxes, deductions, W-4/calculation and tax-rule snapshots, work context, ownership and actual payment proof remain bound. A supported same-year pay-date correction or background tax sync therefore does not invalidate an already-posted disposition or prevent its original receipt from retrying.

Preview-to-confirmation checks still bind the complete live item, period and payment history. A change after review requires a new preview. The existing append-only clearing exception applies only to valid clearing or clearing-reversal events for the same issued check and amount.

Verification can read a complete version-1 snapshot without rewriting it. It first requires every current model column in the original item and period snapshots, then projects only the documented operational exclusions and derives the retained tax year from the saved period date. Missing material fields, unknown versions, changed tax year, altered financial or payment evidence, and incomplete snapshots remain held for review. A future schema change can require explicit review of an older snapshot's column coverage. This compatibility path performs no backfill or production data repair and never changes saved receipt commands.
