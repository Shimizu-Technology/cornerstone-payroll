> **DRAFT: not sent**

[Date]

[Name, title]
Department of Revenue and Taxation

**Re: Electronic W-2GU employee copies, truncated SSNs, and year-end filing questions**

Dear [Name],

Cornerstone Accounting and Business Management prepares payroll and year-end forms for Guam employers. We're adding an online employee service where our clients' employees can view their pay records and a copy of their W-2GU. For tax year 2026, employees will still receive paper W-2GUs as usual. Before we offer electronic-only delivery for later years, we'd like DRT's written position on the questions below.

**Part 1: employee copies**

1. Does DRT accept W-2GUs furnished to employees electronically, with the employee's consent, under the IRS rules in 26 CFR 31.6051-1(j)?
2. May employee copies (Copies B, C and 2) show only the last 4 digits of the employee's SSN? We understand DRT's own copies need the full number.

**Part 2: year-end filing**

3. How does a payroll-processor account on GuamTax.com work alongside the rule of one GuamTax account per EIN? Can corrected W-2GUs (W-2c/W-3cSS) be e-filed yet?
4. Is the 10-return e-file threshold counted across W-2GUs and 1099s combined, or separately for each form type? Tax Alert 23-01 appears to say both.
5. What are the current due dates for 1099-MISC and other 1099s, apart from 1099-NEC, filed with DRT?
6. What is the Guam income tax withholding return called, and what is its deposit schedule?
7. Is the federal Form W-4 the Guam withholding certificate, or is there a Guam version?
8. Will the 2026 W-2GU include codes TP and TT and Box 14b? Do the federal deductions for tips and overtime apply against Guam income tax?

A written reply, by letter or email, would help us most. Thank you for your help.

## Letter 2: Guam Department of Labor, Wage & Hour Division

> **DRAFT: not sent**

To: wage.questions@dol.guam.gov (address from the research brief; confirm before sending)

**Subject: Questions on electronic pay statements, record retention and pay practices**

Dear Wage & Hour Division,

Cornerstone Accounting and Business Management runs payroll for Guam employers. We're adding an online service where employees can view their pay statements. For now, every employee keeps getting a paper pay statement as today. We'd like the Division's written guidance on the questions below before we change that.

1. Is direct deposit allowed under 22 GCA § 3212(b), which says payments "shall be in the form of cash or check"? Are payroll cards allowed?
2. Are electronic pay statements acceptable under § 3212(a)? If so, what employee consent does the Division expect?
3. Which regulation sets the six-year payroll record retention stated in the GDOL FLSA Handbook? Does online access to records satisfy the requirement to keep records "in or about the premises"?
4. Is there a preferred format for meal-period waivers under § 3107(c)?
5. Is there a minimum pay frequency (weekly, biweekly, semimonthly or monthly)?
6. Is any increase to the minimum wage above $9.25 pending or scheduled?
7. Is there a form or format for the § 3213 statement employers must give when wages aren't paid on time?

**For later (ALPCD, H-2B)**

8. Can the monthly report under 17 GAR § 7110 be filed electronically, and how should deduction authorizations be filed?

A written reply would help us most. Thank you.
