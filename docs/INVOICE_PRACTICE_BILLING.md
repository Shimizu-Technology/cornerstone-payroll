# Monthly practice billing

In Invoice Center, open **Senders & logos** to edit a billing profile. Existing profiles retain prefix/year numbering. For a continuing sequence, choose **Plain continuing sequence** and enter the next number (7055 for Dafne's initial setup). **Digits in invoice number** adds leading zeros: Dafne's invoices run "07052", "07053", "07054", so 5 digits makes the next one "07055". Leave it blank for no zeros. A number is never cut short, already-issued invoices keep their numbers, and "7055" and "07055" count as the same number for one sender, whether the number is allocated, imported or typed in. A unique database index (`index_invoices_on_billing_profile_normalized_number`, all-digit numbers per sender) enforces this even when two saves happen at once; the second gets "Invoice number has already been taken". Drafts receive internal identifiers; the official number is allocated under a sender lock when issued. The new-invoice form shows the number the next issue would take ("Next: 07055 (assigned when issued)"). A prefix/year sender numbers a draft when it is saved, so its form says "assigned when saved". Either preview can be taken by another invoice first. The unique sender/number index remains authoritative. Allocation can leave gaps after failures; it is not a gapless ledger.

Add saved services to that sender. A service has a name, description template and kind. `{month}` expands to the selected service month's full month/year. For example, Accounting Consulting with `Accounting Services – {month}`. Add Reimbursement with a pass-through kind for taxes paid on a client's behalf.

Save each customer's monthly fee in **Default rate**, payment terms (Net 15, Net 30 or Due on receipt), and optional payroll-client link. In a new Center draft, choose the customer and saved service. The client link defaults from the customer; for continuing-number senders, service dates initially cover the invoice month and can be edited explicitly. Existing prefix/year senders start without a service period. Once edited, the service period remains fixed across invoice-date changes and reopening. Review the service month before saving. Both the linked client and service period remain visible on invoice detail.

Reimbursements are part of the invoice total, payments, outstanding balance and aging. `line_kind` is internal and preserved on lines and issued snapshots. A product/service name prints separately from the description; empty names retain the original PDF columns. `service_fee_amount` and the **Billed service fees** summary exclude reimbursements and allocate invoice discounts proportionally across fee and reimbursement lines. This is issued billed value, including uncollectible invoices; it is not cash receipts, a credit-adjusted revenue ledger or a historical month-end report. `InvoiceServiceFeeRevenue.call(finance_book:)` groups that value by explicitly linked payroll client and service month. The entire invoice belongs to the month containing `service_period_start`, falling back to the invoice month; it is not spread across months. Drafts and voids contribute nothing. Archived issued invoices and uncollectible invoices still count. Importing an original creates one "Imported invoice" line classified as a service fee, so the whole imported total counts as fees, including any reimbursement inside the original. Imported invoices with no lines at all contribute zero. Before importing originals that include reimbursements for the profit report, split the reimbursement out (follow-up: let the import form take a reimbursement amount). Credits and payments do not change billed fee value. Cash revenue, credit attribution and prorated profit reporting need a separate ledger policy. Unlinked invoices are unallocated; do not infer a payroll client from a recipient's name. `by_recipient: true` splits those unlinked invoices by invoice customer, so client profitability can count them for a Tax client explicitly linked to that customer (docs/CLIENT_PROFITABILITY.md).

**Make recurring** suggests the period after the source invoice as the first invoice date: its service-period start (or invoice date) plus one interval, moved forward whole intervals if that date has passed. The suggestion follows the Repeat and Every fields until the date is picked by hand. For continuing profiles, recurrence creates review drafts spanning the occurrence date through the day before the next occurrence (including multi-month intervals). It updates the description's month, applies the customer's current fee to the first service-fee line only, and derives the due date from recognized customer terms (Net N or Due on receipt). When those terms apply, the form hides **Due after days** and shows the terms instead, and the schedule list reads "Due per customer terms (Net 15)". Other terms retain the scheduled due-day interval, which the form defaults from the invoice's own Net N terms, else 30. Existing yearly profiles retain their source terms, descriptions, rates and scheduled due-day behavior. Continuing-profile reimbursements start at zero with a review flag. Across every creation path and profile, a zero-amount reimbursement requires review and blocks issue until it has a positive amount or is removed. Official continuing numbers are allocated only at issue.

## Invoice email

Each invoice goes out as one email with its issued PDF attached. The default follows how the office sends invoices today:

- Subject: "Invoice <number> - <customer> - <service month>", for example "Invoice 07052 - MoSa's Hot Box, Inc. - November 2026". A service period of whole months reads as months ("October - November 2026"); any other span reads as dates. Without a service period, the invoice month is used.
- Body: "Hello,", a line saying the invoice is attached with the amount due and the due date ("due by December 15, 2026", or "due on receipt"), then the sender's footer note (or "Thank you for your business.") and the sender's legal or display name.

The email is delivery content, not invoice evidence. A blank saved subject or message means "use the default", worked out when the email is shown or sent, so it names the issued number and the current balance. `InvoiceEmailContent` writes the default on the API; `web/src/lib/invoice-email.ts` mirrors it to preview a draft that is still being edited.

- **Email message** on invoice detail in Invoice Center, and the Assistant's **Email Draft**, show the subject and message and can change them for drafts and issued invoices (not voided ones). `PATCH /admin/invoices/:id/email` saves them and records an `email_updated` event. The issued PDF and snapshot do not change. Text equal to the default is saved blank so it keeps following the invoice; **Use default** clears an edit.
- **Send now** and **Schedule email** show the same subject and message, editable for that send only. The reviewed text is stored on the send schedule and is what the provider receives. A schedule created through the API without text sends the invoice's email as it stands when the send is claimed. A pending schedule's text can be changed until it is claimed.
- Each provider delivery keeps the subject and message it sent (`invoice_deliveries.email_subject`, `email_body`), shown under **Delivery history**. Deliveries recorded by hand don't have them.
- Recurring drafts start from the default email for their own number and month. A custom email on the source invoice is not copied, because it names the source's number and month.
- The Assistant's AI preview leaves the email blank (the default) unless staff ask for specific wording.

To send from Gmail, issue, **Download original**, email that PDF yourself, then **Mark as sent (by my own email)**. Recording delivery does not send email. The separate Send now/Schedule email controls use the existing invoice transport; no production email configuration is changed here.

Issued PDFs remain exact preserved evidence. Existing issued documents are not rewritten to adopt new rounding. Legacy drafts recalculate before issue; new lines round half-up to cents and totals sum those rounded amounts. Quantity and rate support at most two decimal places. USD is the only supported currency.

Draft defaults also apply on the server for API and AI-created invoices: omitted terms use the customer then sender, and an omitted first service-fee rate uses the customer rate. Explicit zero rates remain zero. Recognized terms recompute draft due dates when dates or terms change, until the due date is edited manually. Untouched saved-service descriptions follow service-month changes; edited descriptions show a review warning. Void and uncollectible actions require their own reason in a keyboard-accessible dialog.

Recurring fixed discounts are capped at the regenerated service-fee subtotal. The draft retains the original notes and adds a review note; a `recurring_discount_capped` event records the original and capped amounts. This lets the occurrence advance even when reimbursements restart at zero or the customer's monthly fee falls.

`InvoiceServiceFeeRevenue.call` accepts `service_month_from` and `service_month_through` dates, inclusive of their full months, and filters invoices in SQL before loading lines. Report endpoints should pass the requested period to bound history loading; the service currently has no report endpoint caller.
