**[Company name]** now has an app where you can see your pay stubs and tax forms on your phone, any time.

You'll still get your pay stubs and W-2 the usual way. The app is an extra copy.

## What you need

- Your phone (a computer works too)
- Your date of birth
- The **last 4 digits** of your Social Security number
- An email address that only you use

## Sign up: about 5 minutes

**Got an email from us?**

1. Open the email **on your phone** and tap the link.
2. Enter your date of birth and the last 4 of your Social Security number. Tap **Continue**.
3. Enter your email. Tap **Create account**.
4. Choose a password, or use Google, or have a code emailed to you.
5. You're in. You'll see your latest pay.

**Got a paper sheet with a code instead?**

1. On your phone, go to the address printed on your sheet.
2. Tap **Set up your account**.
3. Type the 10-letter code from your sheet, then your date of birth and the last 4 of your Social Security number.
4. Then do steps 3–5 above.

Each email link and each code works **once** and only for **14 days**.

## Tip: put it on your Home Screen

- **iPhone:** in Safari, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen**.

## What you can do in the app

- **Pay:** every paycheck, what you earned, what was taken out and your take-home pay. Download any pay stub.
- **Taxes:** your W-2 when it's ready.
- **My info:** change your phone number and email, add emergency contacts, and ask to change your home address.
- **Help:** who to ask at [Company name].

## Keep it private

- Don't share your code, your password or your email link with anyone.
- Nobody from [Company name] or Cornerstone will ever ask for your password.
- Your pay isn't saved on your phone. You need Wi-Fi or data to see it.
- On a new phone, we'll email you a code to check it's you.

## Need help?

Ask **[names of your managers]**.

- **"It says too many tries."** Wait, or ask your manager. Sign-up pauses for a day after 5 wrong tries.
- **"My code or link doesn't work."** It may be used up or too old. Ask your manager for a new one.
- **"I forgot my password."** On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot password?**.
