Who is responsible for your information

Your employer and Cornerstone, its payroll provider, are responsible for your payroll records. Shimizu Technology operates the platform for them. Each employer controls access to its own records if you use the app for more than one employer.

What we use and why

Payroll records can include your name, date of birth, Social Security number, contact and employment details, hours, pay, deductions, tax elections, and payment details supplied by your employer. We use those records to run payroll, prepare pay statements and tax forms, and show your own records. Where your Social Security number appears in the app, it shows only the last four digits.

We use your sign-in email and account and device information to verify your identity, secure access, and investigate problems. We keep records of sign-ins, document activity, changes, and the terms version and separate delivery choices you accepted. We use contact details to reach you about your account and payroll documents. Emergency contact information you provide is available to your employer.

Who can see your information

People with the appropriate permissions at your employer, Cornerstone, and Shimizu Technology can access information as needed for payroll, support, security, or operating the platform. Service providers host the website, application, database and document storage, manage network and sign-in services, deliver email and provide optional sign-in and product analytics. Information can be processed outside Guam. Payroll or tax information may be provided to government agencies when required or permitted for payroll reporting or by law.

Support sessions

Authorized Cornerstone staff can use a time-limited, view-only support session to see the employee app as you see it and investigate a problem. We log this access. Staff cannot use view-only access to change your information. If support actions on your behalf are introduced, the applicable controls and notice will explain when they are allowed. Staff cannot accept terms, give or withdraw consent, sign documents, or change banking or sign-in security settings for you through this support feature.

Product analytics

If enabled for the employee app, PostHog is used to understand how the app is used. It uses a pseudonymous identifier, hides page text in analytics, does not record sessions, discards IP addresses from analytics, and respects your browser’s Do Not Track setting. Sign-in and security logs are separate.

How we protect and retain records

The app uses encrypted connections and encrypts sensitive fields such as Social Security and bank account numbers in storage. Access is limited by role and logged. Two-step sign-in adds another check where enabled. If a security incident affects your information, the responsible parties will notify affected people and authorities as required by law.

Payroll, tax, account, and audit records are kept as needed to provide the service and meet recordkeeping, security, and legal obligations. How long we keep a record depends on what it is and why it is needed. Former employees may have read-only access for a limited period. Contact your employer or Cornerstone for records after access ends.

View or correct your information

In My info, you can update supported contact details and complete any confirmation steps shown by the app. Submit an address change for your employer to review. To correct your legal name or Social Security number, ask your manager to put you in touch with Cornerstone. Avoid sending a full Social Security number or bank details by ordinary email.

Delivery choices and questions

Pay statements and Form W-2GU each have a separate consent if electronic delivery is offered. The disclosure for each document explains paper delivery and how to withdraw. The app does not send text messages today. Any future texts would require separate consent.

For questions or requests about your information, contact your employer using the contacts listed in Help, or ask your manager. Your employer can put you in touch with Cornerstone if needed. Shimizu Technology operates the platform and works with your employer and Cornerstone on technical issues. Its published platform contact is shimizutechnology@gmail.com.
