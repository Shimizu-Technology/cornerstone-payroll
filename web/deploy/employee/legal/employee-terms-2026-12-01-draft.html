Who can use the app

The Cornerstone Payroll employee app is for people whose employer gives them access to their own payroll records. Your employer and Cornerstone, its payroll provider, are responsible for those records. Shimizu Technology operates the app as a service provider. These terms govern your use of the app. They do not create or change an employment contract.

Your payroll records

You can view or download available pay stubs and tax forms, including Form W-2GU. If more than one employer links your account, choose the employer whose records you want to see. Each employer controls access to its own records. After you leave a job, your former employer may allow read-only access for a limited period. Ask your employer or Cornerstone for copies you cannot access in the app.

Keep your account secure

Use your own account and keep your sign-in details and security codes private. Complete any two-step sign-in prompts and sign out on shared devices. Tell your employer or Cornerstone if you think someone else has accessed your account. Do not use the app to access someone else’s records, bypass security controls, or interfere with the service.

Paper and electronic delivery

Accepting these terms does not give consent to electronic delivery or text messages. Paper delivery continues during the pilot. When electronic delivery is offered, you can decide separately for pay statements and Form W-2GU and can withdraw consent. Review the separate disclosure for paper copies, how to withdraw, and when documents are available. The app does not currently send text messages. Any future text messaging would require separate consent.

Availability and support

Maintenance, connection problems, or changes to your employer’s access settings can make the app unavailable. For help with access or payroll records, contact your employer using the contacts listed in Help, or ask your manager. Your employer can put you in touch with Cornerstone if needed. The Employee Privacy Notice explains how authorized Cornerstone staff can use a logged, view-only support session.

Changes to these terms

We post updated terms here with a new version and effective date. When the app asks you to accept terms, it shows the current version. We record which version you accept.
